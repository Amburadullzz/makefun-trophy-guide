# GTA VI trophy guide and tracker

An offline guide to all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies: every trophy with its official name,
description and grade, a guide in the author's own words with its sources beside it, a
roadmap to the platinum, and the missable ones flagged. The tracker saves progress in the
browser, exports and imports it as a file, filters by kind, and hides story spoilers until
you earn them.

Open `app/index.html` from any static server, or run `node serve.ts` and open
http://localhost:8080.
